## Cfore 2026

Fountainhead School Kunkni, Surat ranks **4th Nationally** — up from 5th last year, two points behind 2nd position.

In Pedagogy and Curriculum, no school scored above us. This is the second year running we have moved up, verified by the Cfore School Ranking.

## Cfore 2025

Fountainhead School ranks **5th in India** in the Cfore Rankings 2025 under the category Co-Ed Day Schools (International Curriculum) — one of the country's most respected school evaluation frameworks.

This achievement marks an important milestone for our community. The rankings place us alongside several distinguished institutions that we have long regarded as benchmarks of excellence, with only narrow margins separating the top schools.

## IDA Awards 2025

Fountainhead School has been recognised at the IDA Awards 2025 for its exemplary work in wellbeing, engagement, and capacity building in the foundational years.

Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) has been an integral part of our philosophy long before it became a widespread priority in education. At the centre of this work is our dedicated wellbeing team comprising seven trained counsellors. Each student receives two wellbeing check-ins every year, enabling early identification of emotional concerns or learning delays.

Wellbeing at Fountainhead is embedded deeply within our pedagogy — tools such as Six Bricks, Block Play, the Leader in Me habits, and Life Classes create a school culture where children feel secure, supported, and ready to thrive.
