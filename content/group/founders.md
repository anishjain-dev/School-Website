## FS Logo Description

1\. The relationship between a teacher and a student, the school / teacher (who represents the spring, the principal source of knowledge) is the spring who imparts knowledge to the student (who is the stream, the beneficiary of the knowledge). The extended hand shows the school / teacher inviting students to learn. The open arms represent the general ambience of the school, where the teachers share a healthy and open relationship with their students, where students learn to be fearless in expressing ideas / opinions, where the teachers work towards nurturing the child and helping the child attain and reach true potential.

2\. The magic is in the bond shared by the one who imparts knowledge and the one who imbibes it. That bond is the principle source of something great which can only be achieved when it is a collaborative effort. To achieve greater heights, the bond has to be strong. The logo symbolizes the bond and the sharing of knowledge

## The Journey

Fountainhead began in 2005 with six students and a belief that education could be done differently. Today it is a network of campuses across Surat, Vapi and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, each authorised to deliver the International Baccalaureate continuum.

## Timeline

- **2005** — Founded with 6 students as "The Fountainhead Preschool"
- **c. 2011** — Authorised for the IB Primary Years Programme
- **2014** — IB Diploma Programme authorised
- **c. 2014** — Ankita Diwekar Kabra conferred the Global Teacher Accreditation Award by the British Council
- **2016** — First graduating class of Fountainhead School
- **2017** — Second Fountainhead Preschool opens
- **2018** — IB Middle Years Programme authorised
- **c. 2019** — MSA-CESS accreditation for the American High School Diploma
- **2020** — Fountainhead School Kunkni established
- **2023** — Ranked Best International School in Surat (Cfore rankings)
- **2024–25** — Great Place To Work certified
- **2026–27** — Malgama, FALH (Vapi learning hub), FWGS, and FASV (Vapi school) join the group

## Our People

### Head of School (HOS)

Bhumika Parmar (HOS) strongly believes that life is one big circle and you get back what you give in life. This philosophy has brought her a long way, both in personal and professional life. She has been associated with Fountainhead School since 2009, and has worked in varied roles as a home room teacher, Team leader and Middle Years Coordinator prior to taking up the current position. Her tenure at the school has given her a rich experience in understanding child psychology as well as the academic needs of the students and has been a mentor to students, parents and teachers alike. She holds a bright academic background with bachelors in Commerce as well as Law, and a Bachelor's degree in education. She has also pursued a degree in School Leadership Management. She has been instrumental in setting up the systems and processes as well as the curriculum development in the Middle Years. She is also leading the IB Middle Years Program in school and ensures smooth administration of the DP program. Her vision of education is not limited to tangibles of academic achievements but goes well beyond that. She has an affinity for reading as well as travelling. Experience acquired in humility with hard work has shaped her personality.
