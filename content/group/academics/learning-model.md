At Fountainhead Schools, learning is not a transaction — it is a carefully designed journey with the **Learner** at its centre. The FS Learning Model is a holistic framework that defines what we believe learning is, how it should feel, and what it should ultimately produce: young people ready to lead with character, competence, and conscience.

## The Framework

The FS Learning Model is structured as a series of concentric rings, each layer building on the one before it and radiating outward — from the individual learner to the world at large.

### Core: The Learner

Every decision at Fountainhead begins here. The learner is not a passive recipient but an active, curious, and capable agent in their own development.

### Ring 1 — Approaches to Learning

Three essential orientations define *how* learning happens at Fountainhead:

- **Goal Directed Learning** — Students set intentions, track progress, and take ownership of their learning journey.
- **Experiential Learning** — Real-world engagement, reflection, and application deepen understanding beyond the classroom.
- **Joy of Learning** — Curiosity and delight are prerequisites for deep learning. We design for them, not despite them.

### Ring 2 — Domains of Development

Learning is whole-person. Four integrated domains ensure that no dimension of development is left behind:

- **Academic Competencies** — rigorous, conceptually rich knowledge and skills across disciplines
- **Physical Fitness** — health, movement, sport, and kinesthetic awareness
- **Socio-Emotional Well-being** — self-awareness, empathy, resilience, and meaningful relationships
- **Creativity and the Arts** — imagination, expression, aesthetic sensibility, and innovation

### Ring 3 — Values

Everything we do is anchored in seven values that are not just taught but lived:

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Integrity** | Doing the right thing when no one is watching |
| **Win-Win** | Seeking outcomes that benefit all, not just oneself |
| **Excellence** | Committing fully — to learning, to craft, to others |
| **Responsible** | Taking ownership of one's choices and their consequences |
| **Respect** | Honouring every person's dignity, background, and perspective |
| **Fairness** | Treating all with equity and standing up for what is just |
| **Sustainability** | Thinking long-term — for ourselves, our communities, and our planet |

### Ring 4 — The Four Pillars

These pillars define the kind of person Fountainhead aims to develop — and the kind of leader the world needs:

- **Character** — the moral core; integrity, empathy, and courage in action
- **Service** — leading by contributing; a commitment to others beyond oneself
- **Competence** — deep knowledge and the skill to apply it effectively
- **Leadership** — the capacity to inspire, guide, and take initiative

### Outermost Ring — Global Outcomes

The two outermost outcomes anchor the model in a world beyond school gates:

- **21st Century Skills** — critical thinking, communication, collaboration, creativity, and digital literacy
- **Global Citizenship** — an internationally minded identity; the awareness and willingness to act for a shared world

## Why This Model

The FS Learning Model makes our educational philosophy visible and coherent. It ensures that every programme — from the IB Early Years at the nursery level to the Diploma Programme at Grade 12 — is working toward the same end: a learner who is grounded in values, capable in action, and ready to contribute to the world with confidence and conscience.

The model is not a checklist. It is a living orientation — one that teachers use to design learning, students use to reflect on growth, and families use to understand what a Fountainhead education is really building toward.
