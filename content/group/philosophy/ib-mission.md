## IB Mission Statement

The International Baccalaureate (IB) aims to develop inquiring, knowledgeable and caring young people who help to create a better and more peaceful world through intercultural understanding and respect.

To this end the organization works with schools, governments and international organizations to develop challenging programmes of international education and rigorous assessment.

These programmes encourage students across the world to become active, compassionate and lifelong learners who understand that other people, with their differences, can also be right.

## Fountainhead Vision

We, at Fountainhead, believe that learning is a life long process and seeks to inculcate in students, parents and teachers the sheer sense of enjoyment of the learning process.

We will provide a happy, purposeful, safe and nurturing environment to help our children develop skills, attitudes and character to achieve sustainable success in life. We aim to scale newer heights in terms of curriculum innovation and teaching methodologies and build an institute of excellence.

**The Fountainhead Ideology**

Fountainhead School shall be the principal spring of knowledge and education that primarily facilitates learning for children. The learners of Fountainhead will have the ideal environment to:

- Develop an undying curiosity and thirst for knowledge
- Attain skills and attitude to equip them for life in the rapidly changing world
- Nurture and maximise their creative, sporting, social, cultural and intellectual potential, including the achievements of optimum academic results
- Be strong-willed, independent individuals who have the capacity to think for themselves and make their own decisions
- Be groomed as leaders of society, developing habits of reflection, mental flexibility and self-discipline enabling them to chart career paths resonating their passion, skills and knowledge
- Be good-natured, responsible and tolerant citizens of a global society who will maintain openness and respect in the face of racial, cultural, religious and linguistic diversity

## Student Mission Statement

**To nurture leaders with character and competence.**

Character is defined as an individual's mental and moral qualities — a combination of values such as integrity, generosity, sincerity, compassion, service orientation, self-regulation and ethics. An individual's character drives them to think and act in the right direction in day-to-day life as well as in unexpected and difficult situations.

Competence is the ability of an individual to undertake tasks efficiently and successfully. Competence means acquiring domain knowledge in areas of interest as well as a range of transferable skills: communication, leadership, people management, research, critical thinking, problem-solving, social adeptness and decision-making — all valuable across all spheres of life today.

The kind of leaders the school hopes to nurture are those who can take charge of their lives, work effectively with others, do the right thing even when no one is watching, and are internationally minded. Such leaders need both character and competence to survive and succeed, especially in the 21st century.
