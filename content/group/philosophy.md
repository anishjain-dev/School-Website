## Our Values

**Character**

Doing the right thing, even when no one is watching. Character is defined as an individual's mental and moral qualities — integrity, generosity, sincerity, compassion, service orientation, self-regulation and ethics. An individual's character drives them to think and act in the right direction in everyday life as well as in unexpected and difficult situations.

**Competence**

Academic and non-academic skills that help students become socially adept people, researchers, critical thinkers and problem-solvers, good leaders and decision-makers — skills valuable in all spheres of life today.

**Fairness**

Treating every person equitably, regardless of background, ability or circumstance. Fairness means applying the same standard to everyone and standing up for those who cannot stand up for themselves.

**Responsibility**

Taking ownership of one's actions, choices and their consequences. Responsible individuals do not make excuses — they learn, they fix what they can, and they move forward.

**Integrity**

Aligning words with actions. Integrity is the consistency between what we say we believe and how we actually behave — in public, in private, under pressure.

**Respect**

Honouring the dignity of every person. Respect is expressed in how we listen, how we speak, and how we include others — especially those who are different from us.

**Excellence**

Striving to be the best version of oneself. Excellence at Fountainhead is not about outranking others — it is about bringing full effort to every endeavour and refusing to settle for less than one's potential.

## Our Mission

**To nurture leaders with character and competence.**

This is the tagline. Every word is intentional.

*Nurture* — not train, not produce. Nurturing is patient, relationship-driven, attentive to each child's stage of growth. It is the opposite of a conveyor belt.

*Leaders* — not rank-holders, not toppers. A leader is anyone who takes responsibility, influences others for the better, and acts with agency. Leadership is a disposition, not a position.

*Character and competence* — the two cannot exist without each other. Character without competence is good intention that achieves nothing. Competence without character is capability in the wrong hands. Fountainhead Schools prepares students who are both: able enough to make a difference, and good enough to make the right difference.

The kind of leaders the school hopes to nurture are those who can take charge of their lives, work effectively with others, do the right thing even when no one is watching, and are internationally minded. Such leaders need both character and competence to survive and succeed, especially in the 21st century. The school's holistic educational programme and the positive, empowering environment are built to foster both.

## Our Philosophy

We, at Fountainhead, believe that learning is a lifelong process and seek to inculcate in students, parents and teachers the sheer sense of enjoyment of the learning process.

We will provide a happy, purposeful, safe and nurturing environment to help our children develop skills, attitudes and character to achieve sustainable success in life. We aim to scale newer heights in terms of curriculum innovation and teaching methodologies and build an institute of excellence.

**The Fountainhead Ideology**

Fountainhead School shall be the principal spring of knowledge and education that primarily facilitates learning for children. The learners of Fountainhead will have the ideal environment to:

- Develop an undying curiosity and thirst for knowledge
- Attain skills and attitude to equip them for life in the rapidly changing world
- Nurture and maximise their creative, sporting, social, cultural and intellectual potential
- Be strong-willed, independent individuals who have the capacity to think for themselves and make their own decisions
- Be groomed as leaders of society, developing habits of reflection, mental flexibility and self-discipline
- Be good-natured, responsible and tolerant citizens of a global society who maintain openness and respect in the face of cultural, religious and linguistic diversity

## Parent Partnership

We believe a child's education is best when the school and parents are in genuine partnership. Parents are expected to:

- Attend Parent Orientation sessions and Parent-Teacher Conferences
- Support their child in self-study and homework — guiding, not doing it for them
- Build a culture of reading and independent thinking at home
- Give children exposure to a wide variety of experiences and places
- Model universal human values: integrity, commitment, respect and tolerance
