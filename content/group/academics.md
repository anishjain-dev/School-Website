Fountainhead runs the full IB continuum. A child can move from their first
years of school through to a university-entrance qualification without
changing the way they are taught — the same conviction about how children
learn, carried across three programmes.

## The continuum

- **Primary Years Programme** — transdisciplinary inquiry, ages 3–12.
- **Middle Years Programme** — concept-driven and interdisciplinary, ages 11–16.
- **Diploma Programme** — six subject groups plus the core, ages 16–19.

Each programme has its own page with the detail that matters to a parent
deciding: age range, how learning is structured, and how it is assessed.
