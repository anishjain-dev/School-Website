At Fountainhead School, child safety is our top priority. We are committed
to creating a nurturing and secure environment where every child can learn,
grow, and thrive without worry. Our dedicated staff and comprehensive safety
protocols ensure that each student's well-being is at the forefront of
everything we do. Welcome to a school where your child's safety is
non-negotiable.

Here are some steps we take to ensure that your child is safe with us:

- Parent pass to ensure the right person picks up your child
- CCTV installed around the school campus and on the bus
- Sick bay team to ensure that the child is taken care of and parents get a report of injury or ill-health
- Well-being counsellors to take care of socio-emotional needs of a child
- Mandatory seatbelts inside the bus
- Bus didis who ensure that the child is safe during the bus ride
- Fire hydrants placed around the school to ensure safety
- First Aid + CPR trained staff
- Professional security and child safeguarding protocols

Read the full [Child Protection Policy](/policies/) for the complete
framework.
