At Fountainhead Schools, every decision about curriculum, timetable, and school culture flows back to one question: what does this do for the Learner? The Fountainhead Learning Model makes that commitment visible. It is a circular, concentric framework — the Learner at the heart, with four rings of intent radiating outward.

## The Learner at the Centre

All learning is personal. The model begins with the individual student — their curiosity, their pace, their potential — and builds outward from there. Every ring exists to serve the person at the centre.

## First Ring — Approaches to Learning

Four complementary approaches surround the Learner directly:

- **Goal Directed Learning** — students set meaningful targets and track their own progress, building self-regulation alongside subject knowledge.
- **Joy of Learning** — intellectual delight is not a reward for hard work; it is the engine of it. Lessons are designed to spark genuine curiosity.
- **Experiential Learning** — concepts become real through action: experiments, projects, field inquiry, and hands-on making.

## Second Ring — Dimensions of Development

The next ring captures the four domains in which every Fountainhead student is expected to grow:

- **Physical Fitness** — a healthy body is a precondition for a focused mind. PE, sport, and wellbeing routines are core, not co-curricular.
- **Academic Competencies** — rigorous, internationally benchmarked learning across all disciplines, delivered through IB frameworks (PYP, MYP, DP).
- **Creativity & Arts** — creative expression develops lateral thinking, resilience, and the capacity to communicate ideas that facts alone cannot carry.
- **Socio-Emotional Well-Being** — self-awareness, empathy, and the skills to navigate relationships are taught, practised, and assessed.

## Third Ring — Qualities of Character

The third ring names the five qualities the school works deliberately to cultivate:

- **Character** — doing the right thing, especially when it is difficult.
- **Service** — turning learning outward, contributing to communities beyond the school gate.
- **Competence** — mastery that is deep enough to transfer to new contexts.
- **Leadership** — the ability to take initiative, motivate others, and accept accountability.
- **Global Citizenship** — understanding one's place in an interconnected world and acting accordingly.

## Outer Ring — Fountainhead Values

The outermost ring holds the six values that govern how the school operates — for students, staff, and the institution itself:

**Integrity · Win-Win · Excellence · Responsible · Respect · Fairness · Sustainability**

These values are not aspirational slogans. They appear in hiring decisions, parent communication, student assessments, and the way conflicts are resolved. They are the operating system of the school.

## The Model in Practice

The concentric design is intentional: the rings are not a hierarchy. Physical fitness enables academic focus; creativity enriches character; global citizenship gives competence a purpose. Each dimension strengthens the others, and all of them exist to serve the Learner at the centre.
