Fountainhead is a family of IB schools. Each campus carries the same conviction about how children learn, the same values, and the same academic rigour — shaped to its own community and city.

## Our Campuses

- **Fountainhead School Kunkni, Surat** — The flagship campus. Early Years through IB Diploma, on a campus designed for children to move, make and question.
- **Fountainhead School Malgama, Surat** — Early Years and Primary Years Programme, rooted in inquiry and play.
- **Fountainhead Wockhardt Global School** — A joint venture bringing the full IB continuum to an international community.
- **Fountainhead Avadh Learning Hub, Vapi** — Preschool and after-school centre. Reading, robotics, and curiosity for children from any school.
- **Fountainhead Avadh School Vapi** — Opening soon, continuing the Fountainhead journey in Vapi.
