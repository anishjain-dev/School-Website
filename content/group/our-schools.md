The Fountainhead group operates six campuses, each carrying the same IB philosophy and the same commitment to nurturing leaders with character and competence. Every campus is part of one group — which means a child who moves between cities does not have to start over.

## Campuses

**Fountainhead School Kunkni, Surat** — The founding campus. IB PYP, MYP and DP. Ranked 4th nationally in the Cfore Rankings 2026.

**Fountainhead School Malgama, Surat** — IB school in Malgama, Surat.

**Fountainhead Wockhardt Global School** — IB school in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, in partnership with the Wockhardt Foundation.

**Fountainhead Avadh Learning Hub, Vapi (FALH)** — A preschool and after-school learning centre in Vapi, in partnership with Avadh. Offers robotics, a reading guild, and a full preschool day programme.

**Fountainhead Avadh School Vapi (FASV)** — Opening soon in Vapi. IB school in partnership with Avadh.

**Fountainhead Preschool Adajan, Surat** — Preschool campus in Adajan, Surat.
