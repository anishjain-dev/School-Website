FWGS hosted its most ambitious Model United Nations yet — three days of debate, diplomacy, and collaboration that drew over 300 student delegates from 22 schools across South Gujarat.

Committees covered topics from climate finance to digital sovereignty. FWGS students chaired four of the six committees, with several earning Best Delegate citations from visiting faculty adjudicators. The conference is now an anchor event on the regional school calendar.
