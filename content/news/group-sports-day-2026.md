Group Sports Day 2026 was a first for the Fountainhead community — all five campuses under one roof, competing in the same events, on the same day. The Kunkni ground hosted over 1,200 students for a programme that ran from the 100m sprint to the tug-of-war relay.

FWGS took the overall points title, with Kunkni a close second. The real winner, as the event committee noted, was the sense of shared identity that cut across campuses for the day. Group Sports Day is now a fixed annual fixture.
