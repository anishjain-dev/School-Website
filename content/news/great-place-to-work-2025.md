Fountainhead Schools has been certified as a **Great Place to Work®** — an independent recognition based on direct employee feedback about trust, fairness, camaraderie and pride in their work.

The certification matters for one reason: a school where teachers feel valued, respected and supported is a school where children flourish. Educator wellbeing is not separate from student wellbeing — they are the same thing expressed at different scales.

The Great Place to Work certification is awarded through a rigorous survey process administered by the Great Place to Work Institute. It covers all staff across the group's campuses, not just a sample.
