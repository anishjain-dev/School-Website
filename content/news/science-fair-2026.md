The 2026 Inter-Campus Science Fair brought together the brightest young minds from our Kunkni and Malgama campuses for a day of discovery and innovation. Over 80 student projects were on display, judged by a panel that included academics from leading Surat institutions.

This year's standout themes — water conservation, renewable energy, and AI applications in agriculture — reflected our students' awareness of real-world challenges. The top three projects have been shortlisted for the state-level competition in September.
