What started as a small after-school reading circle at FALH in 2024 has grown into a community institution. This July, the Reading Guild logged its 10,000th book — a milestone celebrated with an open-afternoon reading event that brought together students, parents, and local authors.

Guild coordinator Ms. Priya Shah noted that many students who joined reading at a basic level are now independently selecting chapter books. The programme is open to children from all schools in the Vapi area.
