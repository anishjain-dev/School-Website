Every result tells a story.

Behind every MYP score, there are years of inquiry, collaboration, reflection, and perseverance. These achievements represent more than academic performance — they reflect learners who have embraced challenge, developed resilience, and grown into confident, internationally minded young people.

Our MYP Class of 2028 achieved a school average of **36.4/56**, compared to the world average of **28.5/56** — a remarkable demonstration of the quality of learning at Fountainhead.

We are proud to celebrate the accomplishments of our MYP Class of 2028 and grateful to our educators and parents, whose partnership makes these milestones possible.

Congratulations to every student. This milestone marks the beginning of many more successes to come.
