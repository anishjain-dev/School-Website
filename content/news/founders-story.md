Every school begins as an idea. Ours began as a conviction.

**Ankita Diwekar Kabra** brings diverse expertise spanning counselling, emotional intelligence, branding, and school design — creating a multidisciplinary approach to education that puts the whole child at the centre.

**Vardan Kabra's** central belief is that Indian education reform requires rethinking assessment methods. As he puts it: *"The reform Indian education needs is not AI — it is the courage to rethink what we assess."*

Because every institution carries the imprint of the person who imagined it first.
