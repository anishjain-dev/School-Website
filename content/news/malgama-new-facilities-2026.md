The Malgama campus took a significant step forward in physical education this July with the inauguration of its upgraded sports complex. The expansion adds a climate-controlled gymnasium, two multipurpose indoor courts for basketball and badminton, and an outdoor circuit-training trail designed with guidance from a certified sports physiologist.

The facility is available to all enrolled students from Grade 3 onwards. Evening slots have been reserved for the campus's competitive sports teams, who begin their regional-circuit season in September.
