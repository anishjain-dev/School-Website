Fountainhead School Kunkni was ranked **5th in India** among Co-Ed Day Schools (International Curriculum) in the Cfore School Rankings 2025 — the first time the campus entered the national top five.

Cfore is one of India's most respected independent school evaluation frameworks, assessing schools across academics, faculty quality, infrastructure, and holistic development.

The 2025 ranking marked a milestone: FSK's consistent focus on learning quality, teacher development, and the IB continuum began showing up where it matters — in an independent national assessment.

The following year, FSK moved to 4th.
