We haven't stepped back from Fountainhead School, Kunkni.

We now work across **six campuses in three cities** — Surat, Vapi, and Chhathai Sambanagar Nagar — as Co-Founders and Directors of Fountainhead Schools.

What began as one school, one conviction, has grown into a group of schools united by the same belief: that education done right changes everything.

— *Ankita and Vardan*
