## Tuition Policy Update for Parents and Students

*(3rd September 2018)*

Dear Students & Parents,

This is to notify you of an important update regarding the school's tuition policy (available on the website). 4 years back, we had changed the policy to take into account that despite the no-tuitions policy, a significant number of students from the Senior School were going for tuitions (for one or more subjects). We changed our stand from a strict no-tuition policy to a position where we don't encourage tuition but we aren't banning them either. We did this to ensure that students and parents don't have to compromise with their integrity by lying about this matter.

However, in the last 4 years, we have observed two disturbing trends:

- Students going to tuition tend to disrupt the classroom more than those not going for tuition making learning more difficult for everyone, especially now as they don't have to hide the fact that they go for tuition.
- Some students & parents choose to go to some former-FS teachers for tuition, as these tutors claim that they are well trained in the FS & IB methodology. This set includes some teachers who spent only a few months at school or were even asked to leave school for poor performance or conduct. In addition, most of these tutors are unethically using old and current material developed by the school. However, for the school, while not an immediate concern, this is not sustainable in the long-term if more of our teachers decide to become tutors rather than teach in school.

Before making this policy change the following background work was done over the last year:

- Understanding from teachers how tuition-going students are adversely impacting classroom learning.
- Collection of student data from Grade 7-12 who go for tuitions and for which subjects.
- Understanding from students and some parents why they go for tuition.
- A discussion with students from Grades 7 to 12 on the pros and cons of tuition.

Therefore, we are making two changes to the policy:

- For those who disrupt the class or the teaching flow, they will be dealt with strictly in a class by the teachers and may be asked to leave the classes and sit in the library or even serve detention if they continue to disrupt the flow of the class.
- Any Fountainhead student who continues to go to a former Fountainhead Teacher or a coaching institute where such a person works after **September 15th, 2018** will be asked to serve detention till s/he leaves the tuition classes. If the student continues to go to such a tutor, they will not be allowed to write any assessments/exams for that subject and will be marked as absent for all these subjects.

Important points and further clarifications have been mentioned in Appendix 3, which all parents and students are advised to go through before filling up the undertaking. Please fill up the permission-cum-undertaking form sent alongside and send it back latest by 10th September. If the signed undertaking is not received by this date, then those students will not be allowed to attend classes till this is done. If we find out that you've lied on the undertaking, the consequences stated above will still apply.

Please note: An update of the Tuition Policy will be posted for PYP students by 30th September.

Regards,
Fountainhead School Leadership Team

### Appendix: Important Points for Parents and Students to Understand

While the school's philosophy and policy are detailed on the website, these points need to be understood thoroughly by parents and students:

- Success in the 21st Century will come to those who have the independence and skills to excel at their choice of career, therefore tuition classes for academics without any specific need or purpose continues to be discouraged.
- Coaching classes for competitive examinations or enhancement classes for arts and sports, speech and drama, language enhancement are not discouraged.
- However, students won't be allowed to go to current or former-FS teachers for speech & drama or, language enhancement (such as phonics, comprehension, writing, vocabulary, book club, reading) or any enrichment or skill-building classes or coaching classes for competitive examinations or for all academic tuitions including English.
- In continuation with our current policy, students may go to current or former FS teachers for sports & arts activities ONLY.
- For all academic tuitions to non-Fountainhead teachers, explicit written permission must be sought from the school.

From our end, we already have and will work further to open the following avenues of approaching our teachers for doubt clarification and other academic support:

- Encourage students to approach teachers during mutually free slots.
- Allow for more students to come early.
- Students can approach teachers in case of doubts during their playtime.
- Students will be encouraged to explore school library resources which include exam preparation material such as resource books, flashcards, study guides.
- Teachers do cater to students' doubts through email and hangouts and will continue doing so.
- Two teachers working Saturdays will be allocated for students to approach teachers for doubt-solving in the school itself before the Term exams.

## Modifications to Tuition Policy Update

*(7th September 2018)*

Dear Students & Parents,

Some parents met us regarding their concerns about the policy update and some parents also emailed us their concerns. What parents expressed:

- While in principle we don't want to send our children for tuition, for various reasons, tuition is useful for students. This is especially true for students who lack the discipline or the environment to study on their own.
- Putting consequences on Grade 10 & 12 students who go to tuitions to former-Fountainhead teachers in the middle of the year may affect their morale and their grades.
- Parents have acknowledged the sustainability issues of retaining good teachers if former-Fountainhead teachers are allowed to take tuitions of our students.
- A suggestion was also made that the school can offer after-school practice sessions for subjects like Maths and Science that need regular practice.
- Parents also suggested that our objective of reducing attrition is met even if the policy implementation takes place in a staggered manner rather than being implemented immediately while allowing students time for transition.

Based on these suggestions and our internal discussions, we are making two changes:

- Current Grade 10 & 12 students will be allowed to go to tuitions to any tutor including former-Fountainhead teachers for this academic year only. Grade 10 students, when they move to Grade 11 next year, will not be allowed to attend classes in school if they continue to go to former-Fountainhead teachers.
- All other senior school students (Grades 7, 8, 9 & 11) will be allowed to continue with whichever tuition till the end of Term 1, this year. Consequences as stated earlier will be implemented from the beginning of Term 2 (22nd November 2018), if they continue with tuitions with former Fountainhead teachers.

The extended date for sending the undertaking will be **14th September** with the following clarifications:

- Grade 7, 8, and 9 and 11 students have to bring the undertaking, the implementation for which will be done term 2 onward (you can write in the comment section of the undertaking that the implementation will start from 22nd November 2018.)
- Grade 10 students have to bring the undertaking, the implementation for which will be done next year onward (you can mention it in the undertaking that implementation will start in Grade 11).
- Grade 12 students need not submit the undertaking.

Detention will be given to any student from Grade 7-11 from 17th September if they fail to bring the signed undertaking by 14th September.

In addition, based on the parents' suggestion we will run a pilot program (paid & optional) from December 3rd whereby current Fountainhead teachers will take practice sessions for Maths. The focus of the practice sessions will be on practicing problems and doubt-solving as well as additional sums for Maths. If the pilot is successful then we will consider making this a regular feature, and also consider it for other subjects.

We acknowledge parents' support in recognizing that the problem of former teachers finding tutoring FS students a lucrative proposition is not just the school's problem but a collective problem that impacts all stakeholders. Being student-centered is at the heart of all our decision-making. The exemption to grade 10 and 12 students for the current academic year has been made so that it doesn't affect their morale or grades adversely for the lack of transition time. For all other grades, being student-centered means working together to solve problems like the requirement of additional practice, inculcating self-study habits, etc.
