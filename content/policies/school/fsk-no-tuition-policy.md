## Philosophy

Success in the 21st century will come to those who have the independence and skills to excel at their choice of career. Tuition classes for academics without any specific need or purpose are discouraged.

The following are **not** discouraged:

- Coaching classes for competitive examinations (NEET, JEE, NTSE, Olympiads, PSAT/SAT)
- Enhancement classes for arts and sports

However, students are **not** permitted to attend current or former Fountainhead teachers for: speech and drama, language enhancement (phonics, comprehension, writing, vocabulary, book club, reading), enrichment or skill-building classes, or any academic tuitions including English.

Students **may** attend current or former Fountainhead teachers for sports and arts activities only.

For all academic tuitions with non-Fountainhead teachers, explicit written permission must be sought from the school.

## Policy Update — 3rd September 2018

This notice was issued to notify students and parents of an important policy update.

Four years prior, the school had shifted from a strict no-tuition policy to a position where tuitions were not banned but not encouraged — to ensure that students and parents did not have to compromise their integrity by hiding this.

However, in the years since, two concerning trends were observed:

1. Students attending tuition were found to disrupt the classroom more than those who did not, making learning more difficult for everyone.
2. Some students and parents chose to go to former Fountainhead teachers for tuition — including some who spent only a few months at school or were asked to leave for poor performance or conduct. A number of these tutors were also found to be unethically using school material.

**Changes made to the policy (effective 15 September 2018):**

1. Students who disrupt the class or teaching flow will be dealt with strictly — they may be asked to leave and sit in the library, or serve detention.
2. Any Fountainhead student who continues to attend a former Fountainhead teacher or a coaching institute where such a person works after 15th September 2018 will serve detention until they leave those tuition classes. If the student continues, they will not be allowed to write assessments or exams for that subject and will be marked absent for all those subjects.

---

## Policy Update — 7th September 2018

Following meetings and emails from concerned parents, the school made two further changes:

**Exemptions granted:**

- Current Grade 10 and 12 students were allowed to continue tuitions (including with former Fountainhead teachers) for that academic year only, to avoid disrupting their preparation mid-year.
- Grade 10 students moving to Grade 11 the following year would not be allowed to attend class if they continued with former Fountainhead teachers.
- All other senior school students (Grades 7, 8, 9 and 11) were permitted to continue their existing tuitions until the end of Term 1. Consequences would apply from the beginning of Term 2 (22nd November 2018).

**Pilot programme announced:**

From 3rd December, a paid optional pilot programme of practice sessions for Maths conducted by current Fountainhead teachers was introduced, with a focus on problem-solving and doubt-clearing. If successful, this was to be extended to other subjects.

## School Support Available to Students

The school provides the following avenues for academic support without the need for tuitions:

- Students may approach teachers during mutually free slots
- DP Maths HL and other students are permitted to come in early for additional support
- Students can approach teachers during break time for doubt-clearing
- School library resources include exam preparation material, flashcards, study guides and resource books
- Teachers are available for doubt-solving via email and school communication tools
- Two teachers working on Saturdays are allocated for doubt-solving sessions before term exams

## Reasons Tuition is Discouraged

| Reason claimed for tuition | School's position |
|---|---|
| Personal attention | Tuition classes are often overcrowded. Teachers at school help students during free slots and before school. |
| Pace too fast for some | Tuition done in advance causes students to lose interest and become disruptive in school. Different strategies in tuition versus school cause confusion. |
| No one to help at home | The curriculum is covered thoroughly in depth at school. Online tools like Khan Academy build better independent study skills. |
| Extra practice for Maths/Science | In MYP, extra practice only helps Criterion A (25% of the grade). The rest requires self-study and conceptual understanding. |
| Factual knowledge for Humanities | IB frameworks differ from what tuition teachers know; constant curriculum changes mean they are out of date quickly. |
| Disciplined study time | Tuition does not build thinking capabilities — IB assessments require independent critical thinking, not memorisation. |

**What students lose by attending tuition:** time for extra-curricular activities, physical fitness, career exploration, reading and skills that matter for good universities and real-world careers.
