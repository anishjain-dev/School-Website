Bhumika Parmar (HOS) strongly believes that life is one big circle and you get back what you give in life. This philosophy has brought her a long way, both in personal and professional life. She has been associated with Fountainhead School since 2009, and has worked in varied roles as a home room teacher, Team leader and Middle Years Coordinator prior to taking up the current position. Her tenure at the school has given her a rich experience in understanding child psychology as well as the academic needs of the students and has been a mentor to students, parents and teachers alike. She holds a bright academic background with bachelors in Commerce as well as Law, and a Bachelor's degree in education. She has also pursued a degree in School Leadership Management. She has been instrumental in setting up the systems and processes as well as the curriculum development in the Middle Years. She is also leading the IB Middle Years Program in school and ensures smooth administration of the DP program. Her vision of education is not limited to tangibles of academic achievements but goes well beyond that. She has an affinity for reading as well as travelling. Experience acquired in humility with hard work has shaped her personality.
