Utsav Oza is an engineer. He has completed Bachelors' of Engineering in IT from SCET and is a gold medalist. He was awarded the Dewang Mehta IT Award — 2017. He has a keen interest in quizzing, drama and poem writing. He competed in state and national level quiz competitions like Youth festival, TATA Crucible, AIU quiz competitions at MEFGI-Rajkot, IIM-Ahmedabad and Nirma University Ahmedabad. He has performed drama and poetry at Asia's largest youth festival Mood Indigo at IIT-Bombay. He loves travelling, listening to music and being with children.
