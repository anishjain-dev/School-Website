Fountainhead School Malgama is an IB continuum campus of the Fountainhead group, located in Surat, Gujarat. The campus supports learners through a connected, inquiry-based learning environment designed for student agency and rigour.

## A Day in the Life

A school day at FSM is marked by collaborative inquiry, student agency, teacher facilitation, and a culture where asking questions is valued as much as answering them.
