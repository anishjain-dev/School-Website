Student life at FSM extends well beyond the classroom. From the sports grounds to robotics, there are countless ways for every learner to discover their strengths.

## Sports & Athletics

FSM's sports facilities include turf from the Netherlands, Cricket Nets, Basketball, Football, Pickleball, Badminton, and Gymnastics. Students can participate in our Holistic Development Programme (HDP) and HDP+, which ensures every student has structured opportunities for physical development alongside their academic growth.

## Robotics

Our robotics programme introduces students to engineering thinking, coding, and iterative problem-solving — skills that carry across every discipline.
