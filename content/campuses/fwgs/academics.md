Fountainhead Wockhardt Global School offers the full IB continuum — from Early Years through the Diploma Programme. Every stage is designed to build on the last, so students move through school as confident, inquiry-driven learners.

## Programmes

- **Early Years Programme (EYP)** — Ages 3–6. Foundation of the IB PYP, built around play-based inquiry and learner agency.
- **Primary Years Programme (PYP)** — Grades 1–5. Transdisciplinary, concept-driven learning.
- **Middle Years Programme (MYP)** — Grades 6–10. Rigorous, interdisciplinary framework developing research and critical-thinking skills.
- **Diploma Programme (DP)** — Grades 11–12. Internationally recognised qualification for university entry.
