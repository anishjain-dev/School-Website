The admission process:

1. A unique link for the online admission form is sent for each child in a separate email.
2. Fill the online admission form and upload documents within 3 days of collecting the admission form.
3. Interaction with student(s).
4. Announcement of admitted/waitlist list (by email).
5. Payment to be made when a seat is allotted.
6. A confirmation email is sent.
7. Admission is confirmed — collect the admission packet, pay the Registration & Admission Fee, and submit undertakings (hard copy), 9:00 am – 2:00 pm.

## Documents required

Uploaded in the online admission form (png/jpg/jpeg/gif/pdf only):

- Child's and parent's photograph (recent, grey background, as on Aadhaar)
- Family photograph (recent — parents, child & siblings in one photo)
- Child's birth certificate (original scan)
- Vaccination card (mandatory till Grade 2)
- Report card of the previous two grades (filled by the current school)
- School undertaking + corporate undertaking if applicable (hard copy)
- No-due certificate (hard copy; not applicable for Early Years)
- International applicants: child's passport and child's & parents' visa copies
- Original School Leaving Certificate once admission is confirmed (else admission may stand cancelled)

## Good to know

Fill all details carefully with names as per submitted documents — changes
need legal documents, and parents must fill the form themselves. The
process has been fair and transparent since inception; any direct or
indirect attempt to influence the admission committee results in automatic
disqualification. Applications after the deadline are not given priority.
