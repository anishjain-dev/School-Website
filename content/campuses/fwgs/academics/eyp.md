## Early Years as Part of the IB PYP

At Fountainhead Wockhardt Global School (FWGS), early childhood education is non-isolated; it is the very foundation of our IB Primary Years Programme (PYP). Designed for learners aged 3 to 6 years, the Early Years Programme (EYP) seamlessly integrates into the broader PYP framework, setting the stage for lifelong, inquiry-driven learning.

By grounding our Early Years within the structure of the IB PYP, we ensure a smooth, continuous learning continuum from a child's very first day of school through to Grade 5.

> "In the PYP, young learners are viewed as competent, curious, and active partners in their own learning."

## Our PYP Early Years Philosophy

Within the IB PYP framework, our Early Years pedagogy is built around four essential pillars:

### 1. PYP Learner Agency (Voice, Choice, and Ownership)

Even our youngest learners are active participants in their education. Under the PYP approach, we honour children's ideas, offer meaningful choices in their daily exploration, and invite them to co-construct learning experiences with facilitators.

### 2. Inquiry-Based Play

In the PYP Early Years, play is inquiry. Structured, open-ended play serves as the primary tool through which children investigate complex concepts, test hypotheses, build language, and develop mathematical logic.

### 3. Social-Emotional Resilience & Well-being

Strong relationships and emotional safety are prerequisites for intellectual growth. We prioritise self-regulation, empathy, emotional awareness, and collaborative group dynamics within a safe community.

### 4. The Environment as the "Third Teacher"

Our indoor and outdoor environments are designed as extension spaces for PYP unit inquiries. Open-ended materials, tactile stations, and rich resources inspire natural exploration and problem-solving.

## Transdisciplinary Units of Inquiry in Early Years

Just like in the primary grades, learning in EYP is organised around the IB PYP Transdisciplinary Themes, tailored to early childhood development:

| PYP Transdisciplinary Theme | Early Years Focus |
|---|---|
| **Who We Are** | Discovering identity, feelings, physical health, relationships, and what makes each individual unique. |
| **How We Express Ourselves** | Exploring feelings, ideas, culture, and imagination through art, storytelling, music, and symbolic play. |
| **How the World Works** | Investigating natural cycles, living systems, materials, and simple scientific relationships. |
| **Sharing the Planet** | Building early stewardship for nature, living creatures, shared resources, and community responsibilities. |

## Core Skill Development in PYP Early Years

- **Language & Communication** — Expressive speech, early literacy, mark-making, storytelling, and phonological awareness through rich narrative experiences.
- **Mathematical & Logical Thinking** — Spatial awareness, pattern recognition, counting, shape identification, and hands-on problem-solving using physical manipulatives.
- **Physical & Motor Mastery** — Gross motor development (climbing, spatial coordination, movement) and fine motor precision (cutting, pinching, threading).
- **Creative & Artistic Expression** — Visual arts, music, movement, and roleplay to stimulate imagination and original thought.
- **Personal, Social & Emotional Growth** — Developing self-care routines, independence, cooperation, and empathy within a classroom community.

## Nurturing the IB Learner Profile Attributes

From their earliest years in the PYP, learners practise and embody the IB Learner Profile attributes through everyday action:

- **Inquirers** — "I wonder, ask questions, and explore."
- **Communicators** — "I share my ideas and listen to others."
- **Caring** — "I am kind and look out for my friends."
- **Risk-Takers** — "I am courageous and try new things."
- **Reflective** — "I think about what I did and how to do it better."

## A Seamless Continuum into Primary Grades

Because EYP is an essential phase of the IB PYP at FWGS, children transition effortlessly into higher PYP grades. They enter Grade 1 already familiar with the vocabulary of inquiry, portfolio reflections, action-oriented learning, and the IB Learner Profile.

## Partnering with Early Years Families

Parents are essential partners in the PYP journey. We maintain continuous dialogue through:

- **Interactive PYP Learning Portfolios** — Documenting individual growth, child reflections, and unit milestones.
- **Parent-Facilitator Conferences & Learning Showcases** — Involving parents directly in classroom inquiries and progress celebrations.
- **Smooth Transition Support** — Thoughtful orientation programmes to make every child's transition into the PYP warm, joyful, and reassuring.
