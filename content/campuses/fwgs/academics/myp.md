## What is the IB Middle Years Programme?

The IB Middle Years Programme (MYP) is a challenging framework that encourages students to make practical connections between their studies and the real world. At Fountainhead Wockhardt Global School (FWGS), the MYP runs from Grade 6 to Grade 10 and is the natural bridge between the IB Primary Years Programme and the IB Diploma Programme.

The MYP provides a framework of learning that encourages students to become **creative, critical, and reflective thinkers** — not just academically challenged, but personally nurtured to prepare for the future and contribute to a global society. MYP Assessments focus on understanding and critical thinking, not rote memory.

> In 2015, the Australian Council for Educational Research concluded: "Students with MYP background were found to outperform students who participated in other middle years curricula in total diploma points and specific DP subjects."

## The MYP Curriculum

The MYP curriculum framework comprises **eight subject groups**, providing a broad and balanced education:

| Subject Group | Subjects at FWGS (Grades 6–8) | Subjects at FWGS (Grades 9–10) |
|---|---|---|
| **Language & Literature** | English Language & Literature | English Language & Literature |
| **Language Acquisition** | Hindi | Hindi |
| **Individuals & Societies** | Integrated Humanities (History, Geography, Civics, Economics & Business Studies) | Integrated Humanities |
| **Sciences** | Integrated Sciences (Physics, Chemistry & Biology) | Integrated Sciences |
| **Mathematics** | Mathematics | Mathematics (Standard/Extended) |
| **Design** | Digital Design | Digital Design |
| **Arts** | Visual Arts (art form selected in Grade 7 remains same) | Visual Art |
| **Physical & Health Education** | Physical & Health Education (PHE) | Physical & Health Education (PHE) |

**Compulsory subjects (Grades 3–8):** Marathi & Robotics

### Core Subjects (Compulsory)

**Interdisciplinary Learning (Grades 6–10):** At least one collaboratively planned interdisciplinary unit each year, integrating knowledge and skills from two or more subject groups.

**Personal Project (Grade 10):** Students practice and strengthen their Approaches to Learning (ATL) skills, consolidate prior and subject-specific learning, and develop an area of personal interest. Students produce a personal and creative outcome demonstrating a consolidation of their learning.

**Service as Action (Grades 6–10):** Action is learning by doing and experiencing. IB learners strive to be caring members of the local and global community, demonstrating personal commitment to services that make a difference to the lives of others and the environment. Learners must meet the 7 learning outcomes of service as action during their MYP journey.

## IB Continuum: PYP to MYP

Students at FWGS experience a seamless transition from the PYP into the MYP:

- Focus on the Inquiry cycle continues
- International Mindedness is deepened
- Learning in the Global Context is emphasised
- Conceptual learning becomes more rigorous
- Approaches to Learning (ATL) skills are built upon
- Action and Service Component is introduced
- Inter-disciplinary learning bridges subject areas
- Project and excursion-based learning extends the classroom

## 10 Reasons the MYP Builds Exceptional Learners

1. **Become a life-long learner** — learn *how to learn* using communication, research, self-management, collaboration, and critical thinking skills
2. **Learn by doing and experiencing** — the Community Project teaches real-world service
3. **The MYP encourages critical thinking** — analyse, evaluate, and consider new perspectives
4. **Explore global challenges** — increase understanding of the world by exploring globally significant ideas and issues
5. **Learn for understanding** — not just to memorise facts or topics and prepare for exams
6. **Train yourself to** organise and plan your work, meet deadlines, concentrate, bounce back, persist, and think positively
7. **Subjects are not taught in isolation** — make connections between subject areas
8. **It empowers you to develop your talents** — feel empowered to prove what you know and earn the MYP certificate or MYP course results
9. **It prepares you for future education** — preparation for the IB Diploma or IB Career-related Programmes delivered by IB World Schools globally
10. **It encourages international-mindedness** — critically appreciate your own and other cultures, histories, and traditions

## Assessment: MYP eAssessment in Grade 10

MYP eAssessment has been developed for students and schools that need external verification of student achievement at age 16. It strengthens the continuum and offers students formal, recognised qualifications.

**On-screen examination subjects** (externally marked): Mathematics, Language and Literature, Sciences, Individual and Societies, Interdisciplinary Unit, Language Acquisition — Written Component

**Course work** (internally marked, externally moderated): Physical and Health Education or Design or Arts · Language Acquisition — Oral Component

**Key advantages of the eAssessment model:**
- Contextualised and authentic
- Inter-disciplinary assessments
- Uses multimedia and interactive activities; assessments in line with 21st-century learning
- Internationally recognised IB MYP Certificate

**How to achieve the MYP Certificate:** The maximum total score for the IB MYP Certificate is 56, with a grade from 1–7 assigned to each required eAssessment. Students must achieve a total of at least 28 points, with a grade of 3 or higher in each eAssessment component, to be eligible for the IB MYP Certificate.

## Is the MYP Equivalent to 10th Standard in India?

**Yes.** The Middle Years Programme (MYP) conducted by the International Baccalaureate Organisation, Geneva, Switzerland has been equated with Grade 10 of an Indian Board for the purpose of admission to higher studies in a school in India since 2013 — recognised by the AIU (Association of Indian Universities).

## Is the MYP Good Preparation for the DP?

Absolutely. The MYP is flexible enough to accommodate most national or local curriculum requirements. It builds upon the knowledge, skills, and attitudes developed in the PYP and prepares students to meet the academic challenges of the DP. MYP topic lists have been created with the DP curriculum in mind. MYP students show:

- Higher mean performance compared to non-MYP students
- Improved performance in Theory of Knowledge (TOK)
- The Community Project especially helps students with the Service component of CAS
