## What is the IB Diploma Programme?

The IB Diploma Programme (IBDP) is an academically challenging and balanced programme of education with final examinations that prepares students aged 16 to 19 for success at university and beyond. At Fountainhead Wockhardt Global School (FWGS), the DP is the natural continuation of the IB Middle Years Programme — the same inquiry-driven, concept-based philosophy carried through to university entrance.

The DP addresses the intellectual, social, emotional, and physical well-being of students. It develops young people who have excellent breadth and depth of knowledge — students who flourish physically, intellectually, emotionally, and ethically.

> The IB Diploma Programme has been recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) as equivalent to Grade 12 (HSC) in India since 1983. IB students can appear for all major competitive exams including BITSAT, SRMJEE, NEET, CLAT, NATA, and management entrance tests.

## The DP Curriculum

The DP curriculum consists of **six subject groups** plus a mandatory **core**.

### Six Subject Groups

Students choose one subject from each group (with one exception allowed):

| Group | Subjects offered at FWGS |
|---|---|
| **Group 1 — Language & Literature** | English A Language & Literature (HL & SL) · Hindi A Literature (HL & SL) |
| **Group 2 — Language Acquisition** | Hindi B (HL & SL) · German (SL)* |
| **Group 3 — Individuals & Societies** | Business Management (HL & SL) · Economics (HL & SL) · ESS (HL & SL, Inter Disciplinary) |
| **Group 4 — Experimental Sciences** | Biology (HL & SL) · Physics (HL & SL) · Chemistry (HL & SL) · Computer Science (HL & SL) · ESS (HL & SL, Inter Disciplinary) |
| **Group 5 — Mathematics** | Mathematics Applications and Interpretations (HL & SL) · Mathematics Analysis and Approaches (HL & SL) |
| **Group 6 — The Arts** | Visual Arts (HL & SL) |

*Subject offering is based on the number of interested students.

Students select **3 subjects at Higher Level (HL)** and **3 subjects at Standard Level (SL)**.

### The DP Core (Mandatory for Full Diploma)

- **Extended Essay (EE)** — a maximum 4,000-word independently researched essay assessed by IB
- **Theory of Knowledge (TOK)** — a critical thinking course assessed by IB
- **Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS)** — a non-assessed but mandatory component

## How is the DP Assessed?

There are two kinds of IB assessments:

**External Assessments** (written exams at the end of Year 2, Grade 12):
- Structured problems, short-response questions, data-response questions, text-response questions, case-study questions, multiple-choice questions (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- Extended essay, TOK essay
- Visual Arts Comparative study, Process portfolio
- HL Essay in English

**Internal Assessments:**
- Oral assessment in languages (English and Hindi)
- Investigations, Reports, and Commentaries in all subjects
- Visual Art exhibition
- TOK Presentation

**Scoring:** The maximum total score for the Diploma is 45 points. Each subject has an achievement level of 1–7 (7 being the highest). 6 subjects × 7 = 42 points + 3 Core points (TOK and EE) = 45 points total. Students must achieve a total of 24 points to receive the Diploma certificate.

## IB Continuum from MYP to DP

Students entering the DP from the MYP at FWGS experience a seamless transition:

- Inquiry-driven and concept-based curriculum continues
- Approaches to Teaching and Learning (ATL) skills are deepened
- Learner Profile is embedded in all subjects
- International mindedness is central to every course
- Community Service (MYP) transitions to CAS (DP)
- Personal Project (MYP) develops into Extended Essay (DP)

## University Recognition

**In India:** IB students can appear for all major competitive exams:

| Exam | IB Eligibility |
|---|---|
| AIEEE / JEE / IIT's / NIT's | IB students accepted |
| BITSAT | 10+2 from any recognised board including IB with PCM/B at 75% |
| SRMJEE, VITEEE | IB students with PCM/B at 60% |
| NEET (Medical) | National exam — students from all boards accepted |
| NATA (Architecture) | IB and CBSE are in the same category |
| CLAT (Law) | 10+2 from any recognised board including IB at 45% |
| NPAT, FEAT, SET, UGAT, IPMAT (Management) | 10+2 from any recognised board including IB |

**Internationally:** Top foreign universities view the IB Diploma favourably:
- Students may receive credits and study fewer subjects in the first year of university
- The DP develops critical thinking, intercultural understanding, and respect
- Students may qualify for scholarships
- The IB programme correlates well with international university curricula
- IB students consistently outperform students from other boards at university
