A reading programme rooted in curiosity — guided by literacy research and
best practices from top Indian schools. Led by Ms. Shivanie Jariwala,
Reading Specialist.

**The collection:** Indian literature, international bestsellers,
award-winning children's books, bilingual and multilingual editions, and
classic and contemporary literature. Age groups: 6–8 and 9–11 years.

**To begin:**

1. Visit the reading library, by appointment.
2. Meet Ms. Jariwala and explore the collection.
3. Enrol and start building a reading habit that lasts.
