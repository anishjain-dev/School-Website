Student life at FSK extends well beyond the classroom. From Khelangan — our celebration of traditional and contemporary games — to robotics, from the sports grounds to student-produced newsletters, there are countless ways for every learner to discover their strengths.

## Khelangan

Our annual celebration of games brings the entire school community together through sport, play, and shared joy.

## Sports & Athletics

FSK's sports grounds give students the space to compete, collaborate, and build physical confidence alongside their academic growth.

## Robotics

Our robotics programme introduces students to engineering thinking, coding, and iterative problem-solving — skills that carry across every discipline.

## School Calendar

Access the FSK school calendar for key dates, events, and holiday schedules.

## Newsletters

Stay connected with the FSK community through our student-produced newsletters.
