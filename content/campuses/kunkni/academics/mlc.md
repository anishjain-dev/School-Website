The Maverick Learning Centre (MLC) is FSK's dedicated skill development programme, built on the belief that education must prepare students for life — not just examinations.

The MLC was recognised as the **Best Skill Development Initiative 2023** at the ScooNews Global Educators Fest in Jaipur, acknowledging its impact on students' practical readiness for the world beyond school.

Through the MLC, students engage in projects, workshops, and collaborative challenges that develop real-world competencies alongside their academic programme.
