## Head of School

**Bhumika Parmar**

Bhumika Parmar strongly believes that life is one big circle and you get back what you give in life. This philosophy has brought her a long way, both in personal and professional life. She has been associated with Fountainhead School since 2009, and has worked in varied roles as a home room teacher, Team leader and Middle Years Coordinator prior to taking up the current position. Her tenure at the school has given her a rich experience in understanding child psychology as well as the academic needs of the students and has been a mentor to students, parents and teachers alike. She holds a bright academic background with bachelors in Commerce as well as Law, and a Bachelor's degree in education. She has also pursued a degree in School Leadership Management. She has been instrumental in setting up the systems and processes as well as the curriculum development in the Middle Years. She is also leading the IB Middle Years Program in school and ensures smooth administration of the DP program. Her vision of education is not limited to tangibles of academic achievements but goes well beyond that. She has an affinity for reading as well as travelling. Experience acquired in humility with hard work has shaped her personality.

## Senior School Principals

**Shezin Siganporia**

Shezin Siganporia is a Chartered Accountant who enjoys taking on new challenges. She is passionate about topics related to Finance and Business Management. She is a social media buff and likes exploring new places. Shezin has been working with FS since 2013. Dancing, reading and listening to music can be found on her hobby list.

**Utsav Oza**

Utsav Oza is an engineer. He has completed Bachelors' of Engineering in IT from SCET and is a gold medalist. He was awarded the Dewang Mehta IT Award — 2017. He has a keen interest in quizzing, drama and poem writing. He competed in state and national level quiz competitions like Youth festival, TATA Crucible, AIU quiz competitions at MEFGI-Rajkot, IIM-Ahmedabad and Nirma University Ahmedabad. He has performed drama and poetry at Asia's largest youth festival Mood Indigo at IIT-Bombay. He loves travelling, listening to music and being with children.
