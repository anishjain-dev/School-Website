At Fountainhead School Kunkni, supporting the whole child — academically, emotionally, and in planning for the future — is central to our mission.

## Career Counselling

Our dedicated career counselling team helps senior school students explore pathways to universities and careers aligned with their individual strengths, interests, and aspirations. Students receive structured guidance on course selection, university applications, and building the profile that opens doors.

## Wellbeing Counselling

Our trained wellbeing counsellors work with every student throughout the school year. Regular check-ins ensure that emotional and social concerns are identified early, and students always have a trusted, trained adult to turn to when they need support.

Wellbeing at FSK is embedded in the school culture — not a service that students access only in crisis, but an ongoing relationship built from the earliest years.
