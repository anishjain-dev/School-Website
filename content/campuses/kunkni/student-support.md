## Career Counselling

FSK's career counsellors work with students from Grade 9 onwards to help them identify strengths, explore pathways, and make informed subject and university choices.

Support includes:

- One-on-one career counselling sessions
- University shortlisting and application guidance
- Entrance exam preparation guidance (SAT, IELTS, TOEFL)
- Portfolio and personal statement coaching
- Alumni connect and mentorship

Reach the career counselling team through the school office or Nucleus portal.

## Wellbeing Counselling

FSK has seven trained wellbeing counsellors embedded in the school community — not a standalone service, but part of everyday school life.

Every student receives **two wellbeing check-ins per year**, enabling early identification of concerns before they grow. Counsellors work closely with teachers and parents to ensure support is timely and tailored.

Key programmes and tools include:

- **Six Bricks** — builds resilience and self-regulation in younger learners
- **Block Play** — structured space for imagination and self-directed focus
- **The Leader in Me** — personal responsibility and collaboration habits
- **Life Classes** — age-appropriate discussions on body safety, healthy relationships, bullying, and gender awareness

All counselling conversations are confidential. Students can request a session at any time through their homeroom teacher or by approaching a counsellor directly.
