faq:
  - q: When do admissions open?
    a: Junior KG admissions open in September–October for the following June. All other grades open December–January. Year-round admissions are also accepted for PYP grades, Grades 7–9 in MYP, and Grade 11 in DP, subject to seat availability.
  - q: Is there an entrance test?
    a: Yes — a readiness test covering English, Math and Hindi, plus an oral interaction. For DP, Sciences are also included depending on the student's career path. The test helps the school understand the candidate's prior knowledge, not to screen out students.
  - q: Do siblings get priority?
    a: Yes. Siblings of current FSK students receive preference. This applies once per academic year — if the process is not completed within the given timeline, sibling preference is not carried over.
  - q: What is the EFT workshop?
    a: The Education for Tomorrow (EFT) workshop is mandatory for all parents seeking admission at Fountainhead School. It helps parents understand the school's philosophy and what partnership looks like in practice.
  - q: Does FSK consider donations in the admission process?
    a: No. Donations are never a factor. Any recommendation or external influence to secure admission results in immediate cancellation of the application.
  - q: What about students with identified learning needs?
    a: Admissions for students with identified learning needs are handled individually. Parents share recent psychological and medical documentation, and the wellbeing and learning support team assess whether the school can provide the required support.

Fountainhead School Kunkni offers admissions from Early Years (Junior KG) through Grade 12 across the IB continuum — PYP, MYP and Diploma Programme. We also admit students to the Maverick Learning Centre (MLC) from Grade 5 onwards.

## Before you apply

Attend an **Education for Tomorrow (EFT)** workshop — this is mandatory for all families. It is where you understand what learning looks like at FSK and what the school expects of you as a parent-partner.

## How admissions work

**Early Years (Junior KG)**

Priority is given to children of FSK staff, then siblings of current students, then students from our partner preschools (The Village Early Learning Centre and Flutterby). If the number of applicants exceeds seats, a draw is conducted. An equal 50:50 ratio of boys and girls is maintained.

**PYP (Grades 1–6) and MYP (Grades 7–9)**

Candidates sit a readiness test in English, Math and Hindi, plus an oral interaction. A consolidated cut-off score is calculated — prior school performance and additional points for siblings, staff children, female candidates, former students, and students from IB schools are all factored in.

**Diploma Programme (Grade 11)**

External candidates sit a readiness test in English, Math, Hindi and Sciences, plus an oral interaction and a meeting with the career counsellor for subject selection. Internal candidates (continuing from MYP) do not require an assessment.

## Age criteria

| Grade | Age by 31st May of year of entry |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| Junior KG | 4 years |
| Senior KG | 5 years |
| Grade 1 | 6 years |
| Grade 2 | 7 years |

Beyond Grade 2, candidates must have completed the previous grade satisfactorily.

## The Parent Admission Committee

FSK has a five-member Parent Admission Committee (PAC) made up of existing parents. They observe and certify the entire process — an independent check that every family is treated with the same integrity and fairness.
