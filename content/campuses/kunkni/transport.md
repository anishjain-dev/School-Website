Fountainhead School Kunkni operates a fleet of buses covering routes across Surat. Transport is managed to ensure students travel safely and punctually.

## Finding Your Route

Route assignments, stop details and pick-up/drop-off timings are available through the Nucleus portal.

- [Nucleus Web Portal](https://parents.fountainheadschools.org/Login)
- [Nucleus iOS App](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/fs-nucleus/id1625806958)

## Transport Safety

All buses are supervised by a dedicated didi (bus attendant) who is responsible for student safety and conduct during transit.

**Bus conduct rules:**

- Students must be at the stop before the scheduled pick-up time — buses cannot wait.
- Only healthy foods are permitted on the bus (fruits, peanuts, brown bread items). Junk food, liquids and packaged snacks are not allowed.
- Students must not litter or throw anything inside or outside the bus.
- Any spill or dropped item must be cleaned up by the student responsible.
- Misbehaviour is reported to the Operations Manager and homeroom teacher. Repeated misconduct results in loss of bus privileges.
